Why Cadence asks for Google access

Cadence signs in with Google so it can show and manage your calendar. Each permission has one job:

  • CalendarShow your events, and create, edit or RSVP to the ones you choose.
  • TasksShow your Google Tasks alongside your events.
  • DirectoryFind colleagues by name when you invite them.
  • RoomsList your organisation's meeting rooms.
  • Gmail (optional)Read-only. Links recent emails to upcoming meetings in your briefing. Only on if you turn it on.

Our promise

  • Your data stays on your Mac. Cadence has no server and we never see it.
  • Your Google sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. Your data is never sold or shared.
  • AI features run on Apple's on-device model, or on Claude or ChatGPT using your own API key, and only when you turn them on.
  • Disconnect any time in Settings or at Google Account permissions.

Cadence's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Read the full Privacy Policy.
